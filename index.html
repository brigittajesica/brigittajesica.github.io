About

I am a PhD candidate at the TUM Institute for Ethics in AI (advised by Prof. Dr. Christoph Lütge) and Tech Lead of the AI Moderation Layer at BMW Group. My work sits at the intersection of technical AI safety and AI ethics.

On the technical side, I develop guardrails and automated red-teaming methods that keep large language models safe once they are deployed in real products. Alongside this, I build benchmarks for evaluating LLM safety and policy alignment, including COMPASS and XL-SafetyBench, and, as part of SPAR, contribute datasets for assessing the AI judges used in scalable oversight.

On the ethical side, my doctoral research examines how organizations govern AI and how generative models can be deployed responsibly in practice. Within the EU-funded AIRIS project, I contribute to the ethics framework for an AI research collaborator in biomedicine.
