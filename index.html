I am a PhD candidate at the TUM Institute for Ethics in AI (advised by Prof. Dr. Christoph Lütge) and Tech Lead of the AI Moderation Layer at BMW Group. I work on AI safety: I build guardrails, red-teaming systems, and organizational safety and compliance frameworks that keep large language models safe once they are actually deployed.

Lately my work has centered on adversarial red-teaming and the security of multimodal models: vision-language models, AI agents, and audio systems. I'm also interested in benchmarking organizational AI governance and the ethics of using GenAI in real products.
